I'm Dennis Zhuang. I founded [Greptime](https://greptime.com), where we build
[GreptimeDB](https://github.com/GreptimeTeam/greptimedb) — an open-source database that
stores metrics, logs, and traces in one engine instead of three.

I've been writing open source since 2008; most of it lives on
[GitHub](https://github.com/killme2008).

This blog is where I think out loud: storage engines, distributed systems, observability, and
what AI agents are doing to the way software gets built. I read a fair number of papers and
write up the ones that change how I think. Opinions are mine, and some of them will age badly.

If any of that sounds interesting, follow me on [X](https://x.com/killme20082) or
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dennis-zhuang/) — that's where new posts show up first.
There's also an [Atom feed](/atom.xml) if you'd rather skip the algorithms.
